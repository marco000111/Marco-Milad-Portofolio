Before you arrive

A few things
you might wonder.

A little more information can make your first visit feel that much easier.

What happens at a first appointment?

We'll begin with a conversation about your concerns, then explain any recommended examination or next steps before we begin.

Can I ask for a specific appointment time?

Absolutely. Let us know your preferred day and time in the form and our reception team will confirm what's available.

Do you see children and families?

Yes. We welcome patients of different ages and can talk through what will help each person feel comfortable.
